# Board design principles

The principles are grouped by situation. A rule applies only to boards that depict its situation.

## Rotation placement

These rules cover player placement at the serve moment, while overlap rules hold:

- When a player stacks behind or beside a teammate, keep them close: about `0.05` normalized apart, never further than necessary.
- Where a player stacks depends on where they go after the serve. Pick the spot that gives them a straight run with no teammate in the way.
- Do not place a non-receiving attacker close to the net when the rotation does not require it. Place them where their approach would start, if possible.
